# Workspace Protocol — Paseo Control Workspace

- identity: `owner={{HUMAN_OWNER}}; version=1; applies_to=control-workspace-root`
- purpose: bounded portfolio observation và cross-project orchestration learning; không engineering.
- portfolio scope: chỉ project keys được Human admit trong `PROJECT_INDEX.yaml`.
- write owner: Portfolio Supervisor là one writer cho `SUPERVISOR_NOTEBOOK.md`; pending proposal có
  exact owner riêng.
- evidence: dùng bounded current facts và redacted episode identity; không raw transcript/secret.
- authority: observation/recommendation only; recovery hoặc Lead replacement cần separate exact Human
  lease.
- routing: project finding quay về exact Project Lead/Human; không direct Peer trong ordinary work.
- heartbeat: `none` trừ khi Human bind exact interval, scope, expiry, budget và cleanup owner.
- acceptance: Control Workspace không issue engineering acceptance cho target repository.
- unresolved placeholder/conflict giữ workspace ở `VALIDATED_CANDIDATE/BLOCKED`.
